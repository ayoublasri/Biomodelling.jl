# Figure legends

**Figure 1. The Biomodelling.jl 2.0 framework.** (a) Each cell runs the stochastic kinetics of a reaction network (mass-action, Hill or custom propensities, explicit promoter states) at its current volume; volume grows exponentially, genes replicate at a set point of the cycle, and division follows a sizer, adder or timer rule with binomial partitioning of molecules and inheritance of promoter states. (b) Population control (constant size, free growth or logistic), the drug and perturbation layer (dose schedules, state-dependent death, growth inhibition, rate modulation, genetic perturbations) and the lineage table that records every cell's ancestry, fate and state at birth and division. (c) Observation models (scRNA-seq with capture, depth, dropout and batch effects; smFISH; time-lapse reporters) and outputs (CSV, Newick, AnnData). (d) Downstream uses: lineage statistics, fluctuation tests, clonal memory scores, likelihood-free inference and benchmark generation. (e) A complete example in ten lines of code.

**Figure 2. The engine is exact and agrees with an independent implementation.** (a) Stationary distribution of a birth-death process (bars: 50 000 simulated cells; line: Poisson). (b) Telegraph model in an intermediate and a bursty regime against the Beta-Poisson law. (c) Bursty protein production against the negative binomial. (d) Kolmogorov-Smirnov distance to the exact law for every kernel (n = 20 000; dotted line: 99% critical value) with wall-clock time per 10⁴ cells. (e) Telegraph model simulated with Biomodelling.jl and with JumpProcesses.jl on the same model; the two-sample KS statistic is below its 99% critical value. (f) Runtime of population simulations against the number of cells (10 genes) and genes (1 000 cells) for the exact and hybrid kernels.

**Figure 3. Growth, division and partitioning generate memory, size scaling and cell-cycle effects.** (a) A single lineage: volume, mRNA and protein of a slowly switching gene, with the promoter-on state shaded. (b) mRNA count against cell volume for a fast-switching gene without replication (points, line: mean concentration × V) and with gene replication at mid-cycle (binned means). (c) Correlation of protein concentration at division between mothers and daughters, sisters and cousins as a function of the promoter switching rate; dotted: one over the cell-cycle time. (d) Memory timescale in time units against the measured cell-cycle time for two switching rates; dotted lines: 1/(k_on + k_off). (e) Coefficient of variation squared of a stable protein's concentration across the population and along single lineages, for increasing partitioning noise and cell-cycle-time noise. (f) Telegraph parameters fitted to cells before replication (one gene copy), after replication (two copies) and pooled; dotted: per-allele values.

**Figure 4. A single resistance gene with slow promoter switching reproduces persister biology.** (a) Population size under continuous, pulsed and reduced-dose schedules (shading: drug present). (b) Kill curves at increasing dose. (c) Population decay rate against dose, with the mean division time of cells born under drug (inset), which is nearly unchanged. (d) Concordance of death fates between sisters and cousins born before the drug, for the memory gene and a fast-switching control, against the expectation for independent fates. (e) Effective number of clones (barcodes) before and after drug for pre-existing and drug-induced tolerance (mean ± s.d. of three seeds). (f) Long-term population growth rate as a function of release period and dose for the two mechanisms. (g) Surviving clones after drug with and without a pretreatment that accelerates promoter switching (mean ± s.d. of five seeds). (h) Fraction of high-expressing cells before, during and after a drug pulse for slow and fast switching (MGMT-like phenotypic selection).

**Figure 5. Ground-truth benchmarks.** (a) Clonal variance score against the known memory time of 40 independent genes, from true concentrations and from sequenced counts; dotted: cell-cycle time. (b) Area under the precision-recall curve of correlation, PIDC and GENIE3 network inference on the same 30-gene network observed at fixed volume, in a dividing population (counts and concentrations) and after sequencing (raw and library-size normalised). (c) Effect of kNN-smoothing and MAGIC imputation before inference. (d) Coefficient of determination of predicted log₂ fold changes after knocking down the five most connected regulators, for a no-change baseline and for propagation of observational correlations.

**Figure 6. Inference with the forward model.** (a) Posterior distributions of telegraph parameters from ABC-SMC on a snapshot of non-dividing cells, with the truth and the exact maximum-likelihood estimate. (b) The same for a snapshot of a growing and dividing population: the naive telegraph fit is biased while division-aware ABC recovers the truth. (c) Posterior particles for the drug death rate and protection threshold inferred from a kill curve and sister fate concordance. (d) Posterior predictive kill curves from the posterior mean against the observed curve.
