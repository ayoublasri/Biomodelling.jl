# Figure legends

![](fig1_framework.png)

**Fig. 1 | The Biomodelling.jl 2.0 framework.** **a**, One cell. Reactions run at the cell's current volume, with propensities scaled so that concentrations are invariant under growth; genes replicate at a set point of the cycle; division splits every molecule binomially while both daughters inherit the promoter state. Inset: exponential volume growth, halved at each division. **b**, Populations under free growth, constant-size or logistic control, with dose schedules and a state-dependent death hazard evaluated for every cell at every step, and the lineage record from which heritability, autocorrelation, memory timescales, clone diversity and fluctuation tests are computed; crosses mark cells killed by the drug. **c**, Observation models turn true counts into scRNA-seq counts with capture, depth, dropout and batch effects, into smFISH spots, or into time-lapse reporter traces; outputs are CSV, AnnData and Newick. **d**, Uses of the forward model: detection of memory genes, benchmarking of network and perturbation inference against known ground truth, likelihood-free parameter inference, and schedule optimisation over the period and the fraction of time on drug (star, optimum). **e**, A complete experiment in twelve lines of code.

![](fig2_engine.png)

**Fig. 2 | The engine matches exact laws and an independent implementation.** **a**, Stationary distribution of a birth-death process (bars, 50,000 simulated cells; line, Poisson law). **b**, Telegraph model in an intermediate and a bursty regime against the Beta-Poisson law. **c**, Bursty protein production against the negative binomial. **d**, Kolmogorov-Smirnov distance to the exact law for every kernel (n = 20,000; dotted line, 99% critical value) with wall-clock time per 10⁴ cells. **e**, Telegraph model simulated with Biomodelling.jl and with JumpProcesses.jl on the same model; the two-sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov statistic lies below its 99% critical value. **f**, Runtime of population simulations against the number of cells (10 genes) and genes (1,000 cells) for the exact and hybrid kernels.

![](fig3_growth.png)

**Fig. 3 | Growth, division and partitioning generate memory, size scaling and cell-cycle effects.** **a**, A single lineage: volume, mRNA and protein of a slowly switching gene, with the promoter-on state shaded. **b**, mRNA count against cell volume for a fast-switching gene without replication (points; line, mean concentration × V) and with gene replication at mid-cycle (binned means). **c**, Correlation at division between mothers and daughters, sisters and cousins as a function of the promoter switching rate (protein concentration, solid; mother–daughter mRNA, dashed); dotted line, inverse cell-cycle time. **d**, Memory timescale against the measured cell-cycle time for two switching rates; dotted lines, $1/(k_{\mathrm{on}} + k_{\mathrm{off}})$. **e**, Squared coefficient of variation of a stable protein's concentration across the population and along single lineages, for increasing partitioning noise and cell-cycle-time noise. **f**, Telegraph parameters fitted to cells before replication (one gene copy), after replication (two copies) and pooled. The transcription rate is plotted per unit cell volume and divided by twenty, so that all three series share an axis and the dotted lines mark the true per-allele values ($k_{\mathrm{on}} = 0.5$, $k_{\mathrm{off}} = 1.5$, and 2 for the scaled transcription rate); the raw fitted transcription rates are in `paper/output/fig3f_copynumber.csv`.

![](fig4_persisters.png)

**Fig. 4 | A single resistance gene with slow promoter switching reproduces persister biology.** **a**, Population size under continuous, pulsed and reduced-dose schedules (shading, treatment window). **b**, Kill curves at increasing dose. **c**, Net population decay rate over the first 30 time units of treatment against dose, with the mean division time of cells born under drug (inset), which is unchanged. **d**, Probability that a cell's lineage survives the treatment (at least one descendant alive at the end of the run) given that its sister's or cousin's lineage survived, against the marginal survival probability, for cells born in the 25 time units before the drug; memory gene and fast-switching control, with the fold enrichment above each bar. **e**, Effective number of clones (barcodes) before and after drug for pre-existing and drug-induced tolerance (mean ± s.d. of three seeds). **f**, Surviving clones after drug without memory disruption, with promoter switching accelerated 20-fold from two cell-cycle times before the drug until the drug arrives, and with the acceleration maintained during exposure (mean ± s.d. of five seeds). **g**, Fraction of high-expressing cells before, during and after a drug pulse for slow and fast switching. **h**, Net population growth rate over a 200-time-unit course as a function of release period (time units without drug between 20-time-unit exposures) and dose, for pre-existing tolerance without and with a fitness cost of the resistant state and for drug-induced tolerance; each cell of the map is the mean of five independent seeds and the box marks the schedule with the lowest mean growth rate; every schedule is run on the same five seeds and compared by paired differences, and the margin over the best alternative with a non-zero release period is resolved in only one of the three mechanisms (Supplementary Note 4).

![](fig5_benchmarks.png)

**Fig. 5 | Ground-truth benchmarks.** **a**, Clonal variance score against the known memory time of 40 independent genes, from true concentrations and from sequenced counts; dotted line, cell-cycle time. **b**, Area under the precision-recall curve of correlation (Pearson, Spearman) and GENIE3 network inference on the same 30-gene network observed at fixed volume, in a dividing population (raw counts, concentrations, and concentrations regressed on the cell-cycle covariates copy number and age) and after sequencing (raw and library-size normalised counts); dotted line, random ranking. **c**, Effect of kNN-smoothing and MAGIC imputation before inference. **d**, Coefficient of determination of predicted log₂ fold changes after knocking down the five most connected regulators, for a no-change baseline and for propagation of observational correlations.

![](fig6_inference.png)

**Fig. 6 | Inference with the forward model.** **a**, Posterior distributions of telegraph parameters from ABC-SMC on a snapshot of non-dividing cells, with the truth and the exact maximum-likelihood estimate. **b**, The same for a snapshot of a growing and dividing population: the naive telegraph fit is biased while division-aware ABC recovers the truth. **c**, Posterior particles for the maximal death rate and the protection threshold inferred from a kill curve and sister fate concordance: the two parameters trade off along a ridge that contains the truth. **d**, Posterior predictive kill curves simulated at the posterior median against the observed curve.

![](fig7_calibration.png)

**Fig. 7 | Calibration to laboratory time-lapse data and validation on held-out conditions.** **a**, Fates over 72 h of cisplatin of U2OS cells present at drug addition, observed by time-lapse microscopy (grey; ref. [@iyer2025]) and simulated by the calibrated model (colours; mean ± s.d. of four seeds); the model was calibrated on 7 and 13 µM and the 10 µM condition is predicted. **b**, Predicted population size at the three concentrations and at 11.5 µM, from two days before the drug to seven days of exposure. **c**, Correlation of death fates between sisters, first, second and third cousins present at drug addition, and between unrelated cells (mean ± s.d. of three seeds), against the observation that correlations extend to second but not third cousins. That observation is reported graphically in ref. [@iyer2025] with no numeric coefficients, so the comparison is of pattern rather than of value. **d**, Mean time to death of cells present at drug addition against concentration (mean ± s.d. of four seeds); division times are not shown because too few cells born under drug divided again to estimate them (at most four per condition). **e**, Calibration landscape: every parameter set evaluated by the optimiser, coloured by the objective it minimised, which is the single-seed training error plus the memory prior and is not the seed-averaged error quoted in the text. **f**, Calibrated parameters.

![](fig8_schedules.png)

**Fig. 8 | Dose and schedule optimisation against clinical regimens.** **a**–**c**, Population size, relative to its pre-treatment value, under continuous dosing, the intermittent schedule of the SWOG S1320 trial (8-week lead-in, dashed line, then 3 weeks off and 5 weeks on) and adaptive therapy (drug withdrawn below 50% of the initial size, resumed at the initial size) for a melanoma-like population with a rare, heritable pre-resistant state, when the resistant state carries no fitness cost (**a**), when resistant cells grow 50% slower in the absence of drug (**b**), and when resistant cells are only partly protected and grow at half speed under drug (**c**); dotted line, 120% of the initial size. **d**, Weeks after randomisation until the population exceeds 120% of its pre-treatment size (loss of control; mean ± s.d. of four seeds, censored at 52 weeks). **e**, The same end point over intermittent schedules of given period and fraction of time on drug after the lead-in, for every schedule evaluated by the optimiser (star, best), for the three mechanisms. **f**, Population size under the standard (5 of 28 days) and dose-dense (21 of 28 days) temozolomide regimens of the RTOG 0525 trial and a 21-day regimen at the standard cumulative dose, with one-compartment pharmacokinetics (elimination half-life 2.1 h, a plasma value; an independent study gives 1.8 h) and stoichiometric consumption of MGMT by the drug, one molecule per lesion repaired, for populations in which 1% (methylated promoter, solid) or 30% (unmethylated, dashed) of cells express MGMT. **g**, Depth of response over six cycles for the three regimens, the two populations and MGMT either stable or consumed by the drug (mean ± s.d. of two seeds); a bar at zero marks a regimen that achieves no net kill (the 21-day schedule at the standard cumulative dose). **h**, Final population size against the number of dosing days per 28-day cycle at the standard cumulative dose.
