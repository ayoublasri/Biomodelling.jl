# Figure legends

![](paper2_fig1_melanoma.png)

**Fig. 1 | Dosing schedules for a melanoma-like population with a heritable pre-resistant state.** **a**–**c**, Population size, relative to its pre-treatment value, under continuous dosing, the intermittent schedule of the SWOG S1320 trial (8-week lead-in, dashed line, then 3 weeks off and 5 weeks on) and adaptive therapy (drug withdrawn below 50% of the initial size, resumed at the initial size) for a melanoma-like population with a rare, heritable pre-resistant state, when the resistant state carries no fitness cost (**a**), when resistant cells grow 50% slower in the absence of drug (**b**), and when resistant cells are only partly protected and grow at half speed under drug (**c**); dotted line, 120% of the initial size. **d**, Weeks after randomisation until the population exceeds 120% of its pre-treatment size (loss of control; mean ± s.d. of four seeds, censored at 52 weeks). **e**, The same end point over intermittent schedules of given period and fraction of time on drug after the lead-in, for every schedule evaluated by the optimiser (star, best point; where several schedules reach the 52-week censoring limit it marks the first of a tie), for the three mechanisms.

**Fig. 2 | Temozolomide regimens of the RTOG 0525 trial with MGMT stable or consumed by the drug.** **a**, Population size under the standard (5 of 28 days) and dose-dense (21 of 28 days) temozolomide regimens of the RTOG 0525 trial and a 21-day regimen at the standard cumulative dose, with one-compartment pharmacokinetics (elimination half-life 2.1 h, a plasma value; an independent study gives 1.8 h) and stoichiometric consumption of MGMT by the drug, one molecule per lesion repaired, for populations in which 1% (methylated promoter, solid) or 30% (unmethylated, dashed) of cells express MGMT. **b**, Depth of response over six cycles for the three regimens, the two populations and MGMT either stable or consumed by the drug (mean ± s.d. of two seeds); a bar at zero marks a regimen that achieves no net kill (the 21-day schedule at the standard cumulative dose). **c**, Final population size against the number of dosing days per 28-day cycle at the standard cumulative dose.
